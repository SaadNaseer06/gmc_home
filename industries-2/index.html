Industries

Technology & UX/UI Research

In the Technology sector, our proficiency encompasses helping tech companies understand their markets and adapt to evolving consumer expectations. We also bring extensive UX/UI research experience to the table, including conducting usability testing and collaborating with designers and product managers to create user-centric mobile applications.

Automotive

We offer research services to automotive companies to ensure they stay competitive and innovative before launching new models in the market. Additionally, we have conducted extensive car clinics in Dubai and KSA, collaborating with top brands like Land Rover, Jaguar, Nissan, BMW, and others. We also have expertise in conducting dealer & showroom performance surveys.

Food and Beverage

In the realm of Food and Beverage, a keen understanding of consumer preferences is vital, and we have a strong track record in this field.

Fitness Industry

In the Fitness sector, we assist brands in gaining insights into consumers' exercise routines and how they integrate fitness into their daily lives. We have successfully completed numerous projects for gyms and fitness clubs, as well as for the development of fitness mobile apps.

Tobacco

In the Tobacco sector, we've collaborated with leading global tobacco manufacturers on a variety of research initiatives. These encompass diverse projects, including product testing, assessing the impact of price changes, conducting nationwide tracking studies, and conducting surveys to enhance distributor relations.

Healthcare

In the Healthcare sector, we specialize in helping healthcare organizations enhance patient experiences, streamline services, and adapt to changing regulatory landscapes. Moreover, we possess extensive experience in handling sensitive topics such as HIV in diverse African markets, along with conducting multi-country projects focused on upper respiratory diseases.

Finance & Banking Institutions

We offer data-driven solutions for financial institutions to enhance customer satisfaction and grow their businesses. We have conducted mystery shopping studies to evaluate the performance of bank branches, U&A studies to understand consumers’ banking needs, and usability testing research to facilitate mobile banking, among others.

Hospitality

In the Hospitality sector, our valuable insights empower businesses to customize their offerings to align with the preferences and demands of both travelers and local clientele. For instance, we've assisted hotels in optimizing their amenities based on guest feedback, helping restaurants create menus that cater to diverse tastes, and enabling tour companies like dnata to design experiences that captivate tourists and locals alike. We have also worked with top airlines, Emirates Airlines & Etihad Airways, to understand their customers’ needs.

Social Research

With a wealth of experience in social research, we have partnered with diverse clients on an array of projects within the social sector. Our work has ranged from delving into marginalized communities' realities to crafting effective STEM curricula. Throughout, our focus has remained steadfast: identifying knowledge gaps and providing actionable insights. After all, it's all about positive social change, something we personally believe in.

Government & Public Sector

In the Public Sector, we have worked on numerous research projects for ministries in Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, and Qatar, tackling a wide range of subjects. These projects have encompassed diverse areas such as enhancing the Hajj & Umrah journey experience, boosting engagement levels among Qataris for the FIFA World Cup 2022, and driving improvements at DEWA and Happiness Centers, among others.

QSR – Quick Service Restaurants

In the Quick Service Restaurant (QSR) sector, we have undertaken numerous projects for various establishments such as pizza parlors, coffee brands, and donut brands. Our focus has been to gain deep insights into their customer base, pinpoint unmet needs and identify opportunities for expanding into new business verticals. Our insights & recommendations have successfully resulted in increased sales and a greater market share for these businesses.
